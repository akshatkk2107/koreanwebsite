Korea × India at a glance

A partnership with momentum.

India and South Korea established diplomatic ties in 1973. In 2010, the CEPA trade agreement opened the door wider, and today bilateral trade is above 27 billion dollars.

Today, more than 700 Korean companies operate in India. They have brought over 21 billion dollars in foreign direct investment and created more than 100,000 direct jobs.
